health in Perai.

Event date · 27 September 2026

Ronin Security Services (M) Sdn. Bhd. received a certificate of appreciation for its contribution to the Klinik Kesihatan Perai Health Carnival.

A contribution to the local community

The Panel Penasihat Klinik Kesihatan Perai recognised Ronin’s contribution to the health carnival held on 27 September 2026. The certificate expresses appreciation and thanks to Ronin Security Services for its support.

We are pleased to share this recognition with our team, clients and community, and thank the organisers for their acknowledgement.
